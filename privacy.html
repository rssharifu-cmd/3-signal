Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026 · Effective: September 14, 2026 · Sharflow — AI Website Watchdog

Summary at a Glance

Sharflow is an AI Website Watchdog that monitors website performance and search visibility. When you authorize Google Search Console via Google OAuth, we access read-only search metrics (such as clicks, impressions, and query rankings) to detect meaningful changes and deliver focused alert briefs. We never see, request, or store your Google password. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your proprietary search data to train public AI models.

1. What Sharflow Is

Sharflow ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Sharflow — AI Website Watchdog platform (the "Service"). Sharflow is designed to help website owners and teams track website performance, monitor organic search visibility trends, and detect critical anomalies (such as sudden traffic drops, indexing issues, or momentum shifts).

Instead of requiring users to check complex analytics dashboards every day, Sharflow watches selected signals in the background and delivers concise alerts and summaries only when something requires attention.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide, maintain, and improve our website monitoring services. The categories of information we collect include:

A. Account Information

When you create an account on Sharflow, we collect:

  • Name: Used to personalize your account and alert notifications.
  • Email Address: Used as your unique account identifier, for authentication, password recovery, and sending transactional alert briefs and welcome emails.
  • Password Hash: When you register with an email and password, we process your password using the industry-standard bcrypt cryptographic hashing algorithm. We never store or have access to your plain-text password.
  • Account Metadata: Plan type (e.g., starter or trial), creation date, and last update timestamp.

B. Website Information Provided by You

During onboarding or through your dashboard settings, you provide website configuration details:

  • Website URL: The address of the primary website you wish to monitor.
  • Website Type: The category of your web property (e.g., E-commerce, Blog/Content, SaaS, Agency, Portfolio).
  • Website Purpose & Goals: Your primary business or website objectives (e.g., customer acquisition, search traffic growth, brand presence).
  • Monitoring Priorities: Areas of primary focus (e.g., search visibility, core pages, traffic momentum).
  • Important Pages: Specific high-value URLs or page sections you designate for closer observation.

C. Google Search Console Data (When Connected by the User)

To provide automated search visibility monitoring, Sharflow offers integration with Google Search Console via official Google APIs. Connecting Google Search Console is entirely voluntary and initiated only upon your explicit request.

Important Notice on Google OAuth & Credentials:
  • Sharflow does NOT collect, request, or store your Google password.
  • Authentication is handled entirely on Google's secure OAuth 2.0 servers.
  • Sharflow receives only authorized access and refresh tokens issued by Google after you approve access on Google's consent screen.

When authorized, Sharflow accesses the following read-only Google Search Console data for the website properties you manage:

  • Search Performance Metrics: Aggregate search clicks, total impressions, average click-through rate (CTR), and average search ranking position.
  • Query Performance: Search queries driving organic traffic to your site and their position trends over time.
  • Page-Level Performance: Search metrics grouped by individual landing pages to identify rising or declining URLs.
  • Site Property List: The list of Search Console verified website properties associated with your Google account so you can select which site to monitor.

D. Technical & Operational Information

When you access Sharflow, our servers automatically log standard technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device characteristics, operating system, and request timestamps. This data is used solely for security auditing, preventing unauthorized access, rate limiting, and troubleshooting technical errors.

3. How Google OAuth Authorization Works

Sharflow connects to your Google Search Console using the standard Google OAuth 2.0 authorization protocol. Here is how the flow operates:

  1. You click to connect Google Search Console in the Sharflow application.
  2. You are redirected directly to Google's official sign-in and consent dialog (accounts.google.com).
  3. Google displays the exact permissions requested (e.g., read-only access to your Search Console data).
  4. If you grant permission, Google securely issues an OAuth token directly to Sharflow's application server.
  5. Sharflow uses this token to retrieve necessary performance metrics for your selected domain.

Limited Scope: Sharflow requests only the minimal scope required to read Search Console property data (e.g., webmasters.readonly). Sharflow does not request or receive access to your Gmail, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Ads, or any other personal Google products.

4. Why We Use the Data & Purpose of Processing

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

  • Delivering the Core Watchdog Service: Comparing your search metrics across timeframes to detect drops in search visibility, spot positive ranking momentum, and surface notable query shifts.
  • Generating Alert Briefs: Formulating concise, human-readable notifications regarding your website's health, sent via email or displayed in your dashboard.
  • Account & Security Administration: Authenticating your login sessions, issuing secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT), and protecting against unauthorized access.
  • Service Communications: Sending essential service notifications, including account setup confirmations, password reset instructions, and monitoring status updates.
What We DO NOT Do With Your Data:
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or search metrics to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.
  • We do not use your private Google Search Console performance data to train publicly accessible machine learning or AI models.
  • We do not serve ads or monetize your website data through advertising networks.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Sharflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically:

  • We only use Google Search Console data to provide user-facing website performance monitoring and alert briefs.
  • We do not transfer Google user data to third parties unless necessary to provide the service (e.g., trusted cloud database storage), comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger or acquisition with explicit user notice.
  • We do not use Google Search Console data for serving advertisements.
  • Humans do not read your raw Google Search Console data unless you provide explicit consent to resolve a technical support request, or as required by law.

6. How Data Is Stored & Protected

We implement industry-standard technical measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Transport Security: All data transmitted between your browser, our application servers, and external APIs (such as Google and MongoDB) is protected with encrypted HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS).
  • Authentication & Session Tokens: User sessions are managed using digitally signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) authenticated with secure environment secrets.
  • Password Security: Passwords are cryptographically salted and hashed using bcrypt before being stored. Plain-text passwords are never recorded.
  • Database Security: Data is hosted in secured MongoDB Atlas clusters protected by firewalls, access controls, and IP access restrictions.

Security Notice: While we implement diligent administrative and technical controls to safeguard your data, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage system is 100% secure. We cannot warrant or guarantee absolute security.

Revoking Google OAuth Permissions

You can immediately revoke Sharflow's access to your Google account at any time without contacting us:

  1. Visit your Google Account permissions page at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  2. Locate Sharflow in the list of third-party apps with account access.
  3. Click "Remove Access".

Once revoked, Google will invalidate our access tokens and Sharflow will no longer be able to fetch any data from your Google Search Console.
