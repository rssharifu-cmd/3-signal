Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Effective date: September 21, 2026 · Sharflow — AI Website Watchdog

Summary at a Glance

Sharflow is an AI Website Watchdog that monitors website performance and search visibility. When you connect Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, or Bing Webmaster Tools, we access authorized reporting metrics, dimensions, and derived findings to detect meaningful changes and deliver concise intelligence alerts. We never request or receive your Google or Microsoft account passwords; if you choose Sharflow email/password login, we process that password solely to authenticate you and store a cryptographic password hash. All stored OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Sharflow never sells your data, never serves ads, and does not train its own AI models on your Google data.

1. Who We Are & Scope of Service

Sharflow ("we", "us", or "our") operates the Sharflow — AI Website Watchdog platform available at https://sharflow.online. Sharflow is operated by Shariful Islam, an individual operator based in Saudi Arabia.

Sharflow provides automated website health and search performance monitoring. Instead of requiring you to manually check analytics dashboards every day, Sharflow watches performance signals in the background and sends alert briefs only when meaningful shifts, anomalies, or momentum changes occur.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information required to deliver automated monitoring, authenticate your account, and operate the platform:

A. Account, Identity, and Profile Information

  • Contact & Account Details: Display name and email address, used as your unique login identifier, for transactional monitoring notifications, and for account communications.
  • Google Sign-In Identity: If you choose "Continue with Google", Sharflow verifies your Google ID token and stores your email address, display name, and Google account identifier (googleSub). Sharflow never requests or receives your Google account password.
  • Optional Sharflow Email/Password Login: If you choose to create or sign in with a Sharflow email and password, Sharflow sends a time-limited verification link to confirm email ownership, processes the password you choose solely to authenticate your account, and stores a salted cryptographic password hash. Plain-text passwords are never stored.

B. Website Monitoring Configuration

  • Monitoring Preferences: Primary website URL, site category and purpose, monitoring priorities, important pages (if provided), tracked competitor URLs (if provided), and email notification preferences.
  • Operational State: Status flags and timestamps recording when your website was last audited and your trial or subscription state.

C. Connected Datasource Information & OAuth Credentials

  • Provider Identification: Connected provider name (Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, or Bing Webmaster Tools), selected property or site ID, property name, and property URL.
  • Granted Scopes: The list of OAuth permissions granted during the provider authorization step.
  • Encrypted OAuth Refresh Tokens: OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption with a unique 12-byte initialization vector (IV) and authentication tag. Refresh tokens are never stored in plain text and are never returned to client browsers.
  • Account Synchronization Metadata: Associated provider email address (retrieved via Google identity scopes) and timestamps of the most recent synchronization.

D. Reporting Metrics, Dimensions, and Page/Query Data

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) Metrics & Dimensions: Property metadata (accountSummaries); daily traffic and engagement counts and averages (sessions, total users, new users, derived returning users, screen page views, engagement rate, user engagement duration, and bounce rate); e-commerce and conversion metrics and events (purchases, purchase revenue, items viewed, added to cart, and checked out, plus event names, counts, and values); traffic attribution (sessionSource, sessionMedium, sessionCampaignName, including AI-platform referral classification); landing-page paths including query strings (landingPagePlusQueryString, or pagePath fallback); and device category and country breakdowns.
  • Google Search Console Metrics, Dimensions & URL Inspection: Verified site entries (siteUrl, permissionLevel); daily search performance metrics (clicks, impressions, click-through rate, and average position); top search query strings and top page URLs; and, for eligible plans, weekly URL Inspection checks (up to 10 selected URLs from your site root, configured important pages, top Search Console pages, or sitemap.xml) retrieving indexing verdicts, coverage, robots.txt state, indexing/fetch state, last crawl time, canonical URLs, crawl user agent, and mobile usability verdict/issues.
  • Bing Webmaster Tools Metrics & Dimensions: Verified site list, rank and traffic stats (clicks, impressions, average position), search query statistics, crawl statistics, and reported crawl issues.
  • Identifiability Notice: While Sharflow retrieves aggregate reporting statistics rather than individual visitor profiles, page URLs, landing-page URLs with query strings, and search query strings may contain personal or identifying information (such as names, email parameters, order or session tokens, or specific search terms). Accordingly, Sharflow does not treat URL or query-string data as anonymous.

E. Derived Monitoring Findings and Reports

  • Derived Audit Results: Historical records of period-over-period comparisons, deterministically detected anomaly findings, priority rankings, rule-based diagnostic suggestions, and executive summaries stored in your account's intelligence reports.

F. Operational, Delivery, and Billing Records

  • Delivery Records & Send Guards: Scheduler delivery claims, cached report/email preparation payloads, Resend idempotency keys, and execution logs (daily_deliveries, legacy delivery_logs, and email_send_guards) used to ensure reliable delivery and prevent duplicate notifications.
  • Subscription & Billing Records: Plan and 14-day no-card app trial timestamps, server-side checkout binding hashes, and Lemon Squeezy customer, subscription, variant, status, renewal/expiry, invoice refund, and billing event ledger records (billing_events) used to synchronize paid access.

G. Temporary OAuth, Verification, and Rate-Limit State

  • State Verification & Rate-Limit Records: Signed state tokens, browser state cookies (sharflow_oauth_attempt), and temporary oauth_attempts records created with a 20-minute expiration timestamp when you initiate an OAuth connection. Completed or verified callbacks atomically delete the matching oauth_attempts record. Sharflow also stores hashed rate-limit counters (auth_rate_limits) with expiration windows and time-limited email verification challenge hashes to protect account security.

3. Exact OAuth Scopes Requested

Sharflow requests the following permissions to retrieve search and analytics metrics from your connected accounts:

Google APIs:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/webmasters.readonly
    Purpose: Read-only access to Google Search Console property listings, search performance metrics (clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, queries, and pages), and URL Inspection diagnostics to monitor organic visibility and indexing issues.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/analytics.readonly
    Purpose: Read-only access to Google Analytics 4 account summaries and reporting data (traffic, engagement, conversion/e-commerce events, revenue, traffic sources/campaigns, landing-page URLs with query strings, device category, and country) to monitor website and store performance.
  • openid, email, profile
    Purpose: Standard Google Identity scopes used to identify which Google account you are connecting (such as displaying the connected Google email address in your dashboard). These identity scopes identify the Google account only and do not independently prove website ownership.

Microsoft Bing Webmaster Tools:

  • webmaster.manage
    Purpose: The OAuth scope required by Microsoft to query the Bing Webmaster API. Although Microsoft labels this scope "manage", Sharflow uses this access solely to perform read queries for verified sites, search performance (clicks, impressions, queries), crawl stats, and crawl issues. Sharflow does not modify your Bing Webmaster settings, submit disavow files, or alter site configurations.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

Sharflow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In accordance with the Limited Use requirements:

  • Specific Use Only: We use Google Search Console and Google Analytics data strictly to provide and improve user-facing website performance monitoring, trend analysis, and alert reports requested by you.
  • Third-Party Processing & Transfers: We do not sell Google user data, nor do we transfer it to third parties, except as strictly necessary to provide the service (such as storing encrypted tokens, snapshots, and reports in our managed database MongoDB Atlas, delivering requested alert emails via Resend—see Sections 6 and 7), to comply with applicable laws, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice.
  • No Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, personalized marketing, or interest-based targeting.
  • No Model Training by Sharflow: Sharflow does not use Google Search Console or Google Analytics data to train its own machine learning or artificial intelligence models. Monitoring reports are generated locally as described in Section 6.
  • Restricted Human Access: No humans read your raw Google performance data unless: (a) you provide explicit consent to resolve a specific technical support issue, (b) it is required for security investigations or legal compliance, or (c) data is aggregated and anonymized for internal system performance auditing.

5. OAuth Token Security & Storage

We employ defense-in-depth measures to secure connected OAuth credentials:

  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: All OAuth refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. Each token is encrypted with a unique 12-byte initialization vector (IV) and verified with an authentication tag (iv:authTag:ciphertext) using a secure 32-byte key derived from server environment variables.
  • Transient Memory Decryption: Refresh tokens are decrypted in server memory only when automated background routines or on-demand requests query authorized APIs to refresh access tokens and fetch performance metrics.
  • Zero Client Exposure: OAuth refresh tokens and client secrets are never included in API responses sent to browser clients and are never written to application logs.
  • Encrypted Transport: All communication with Google, Microsoft, and database servers occurs over encrypted HTTPS / TLS channels where technically supported.

6. Rule-Based Report Processing

Sharflow generates monitoring reports using server-side code. It compares authorized metrics across complete reporting periods, applies configured anomaly thresholds, ranks findings, and produces template-based explanations and suggested checks. Suggestions are possible explanations, not proven causes.

Customer analytics, revenue metrics, page URLs, search queries, and monitoring findings are not sent to Google Gemini or external research services during report generation. Sharflow does not use connected Google data to train AI models. Authorized metrics still pass through the hosting, database, source APIs, and requested email delivery services described below.

8. Data Retention

Sharflow retains information as necessary to operate the Service and maintain historical monitoring continuity:

  • Account and Profile Information: Retained while your account remains active, or until a verified account deletion request is completed.
  • Encrypted OAuth Refresh Tokens: Stored in datasources records. Disconnecting a single source in Sharflow clears the stored encrypted refresh token and selected property on that specific datasource record only; because Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 can share an encrypted Google refresh token across both Google datasource records, an encrypted refresh token remains stored on the other Google datasource record until you disconnect both Google sources in Sharflow or complete verified account deletion. Separately, revoking Sharflow's access in your Google Account or Microsoft Account settings invalidates Sharflow's OAuth authorization at the provider, but does not automatically erase stored encrypted token records from Sharflow's database.
  • Historical Snapshots, Inspections, and Reports: metric_snapshots, url_inspections, and intelligence_reports remain stored in the database after you disconnect a data source or cancel a subscription so historical baselines and past reports remain available unless you request account deletion.
  • Temporary Security, Delivery, and Billing Records: Completed OAuth callbacks atomically delete matching oauth_attempts records; uncompleted OAuth attempts, rate-limit counters (auth_rate_limits), email verification challenges, and temporary checkout bindings carry application expiration timestamps (and expire automatically where database time-to-live indexes are configured). Delivery deduplication records (daily_deliveries / legacy delivery_logs), email_send_guards, and billing_events records are retained as needed for duplicate-send prevention, billing reconciliation, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Where automated encrypted backups are enabled, backup archives are retained in private object storage according to the configured storage lifecycle.

9. Disconnecting Datasources, Cancelling Billing & Deleting Your Account

Each control action has a distinct effect in Sharflow:

A. Disconnecting a Datasource in Sharflow

You can disconnect an integration in your dashboard settings. Disconnecting clears the stored encrypted refresh token and selected property on that specific provider's datasource record in Sharflow, stopping monitoring queries for that provider. Disconnecting does not automatically call Google or Microsoft's remote OAuth revocation endpoints, does not clear a shared Google refresh token stored on another connected Google service record unless you disconnect both Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4, and does not delete previously stored metric snapshots, URL inspections, or intelligence reports.

B. Revoking Access Directly with Google or Microsoft

To revoke Sharflow's OAuth permission directly at the provider level at any time:

  • Google: Visit Google Account Third-Party Access, select Sharflow, and click "Remove Access".
  • Microsoft: Visit Microsoft Account Privacy Consent to revoke Bing Webmaster permissions.

Revoking access at Google or Microsoft immediately invalidates Sharflow's OAuth authorization at the provider, but does not automatically erase encrypted refresh token records or historical reports stored in Sharflow's database. To clear stored tokens from Sharflow's datasource records, disconnect each connected source in your Sharflow dashboard (both Google Search Console and Google Analytics 4 if both were connected) or request verified account deletion below.

C. Cancelling a Paid Subscription

Cancelling a paid subscription stops future recurring billing charges and retains paid access until the subscription expiry date shown in the billing portal (see our Terms of Service and Refund Policy). Cancelling billing does not delete your Sharflow account, connected datasource records, or historical reports.

D. Account & Data Deletion Requests (Manual Verification)

Sharflow does not currently provide a self-service account deletion button in the dashboard. To request deletion of your Sharflow account and associated data, email hello@sharflow.online from your registered email address with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Once we verify your account ownership, we will manually delete your user profile, datasource records and encrypted tokens, temporary OAuth state records, metric snapshots, URL inspection records, and intelligence reports, subject to limited retention of billing, duplicate-prevention, security, or operational records required for payment reconciliation, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.
