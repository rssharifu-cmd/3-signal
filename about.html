About Sharflow

You run your business. Sharflow watches your website.

What Sharflow Is

Sharflow is an automated AI Website Watchdog designed for busy founders, site owners, and growth teams who want dependable website monitoring without dashboard fatigue.

Traditional analytics platforms require you to log in every morning, navigate complicated charts, and dig through hundreds of metrics to see if something changed. Sharflow takes the opposite approach: it quietly monitors your search visibility and website performance in the background, analyzing official performance signals and alerting you only when meaningful shifts or anomalies require your attention.

Who Runs Sharflow

Sharflow is operated by Shariful Islam, an individual operator based in Saudi Arabia.

We build Sharflow around a simple premise: website monitoring should be respectful of your time, strictly private with your data, and completely transparent about how performance signals are processed. We never sell user data, we do not monetize via advertisements, and we never use your private data to train public AI models.

How It Works

Step 1

Connect Official Read-Only Sources

Securely connect your website properties using official OAuth integrations (Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, or Bing Webmaster Tools). Sharflow only requests minimal read-only permissions and never accesses passwords or sensitive personal data.

Step 2

Continuous Signal Monitoring

In the background, Sharflow evaluates organic impressions, clicks, search query rankings, and engagement trends. It compares recent data against historical baselines to detect genuine anomalies, momentum changes, or sudden visibility drops.

Step 3

Actionable Alerts When It Matters

If a notable shift occurs, Sharflow generates a concise intelligence briefing summarizing what happened, why it matters, and which pages or queries to review—delivered straight to your inbox so you can act immediately.
