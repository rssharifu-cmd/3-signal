Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 3, 2026 · Version 1.3 · Pre-launch Payment Status

Pre-launch Payment Status

Sharflow is in pre-launch. Paid checkout and recurring billing are not available. Lemon Squeezy account approval is complete, but products, prices and checkout integration are not configured. Paddle is not approved for use and is not available for Sharflow payments.

1. Payments and Subscriptions

Sharflow is not currently accepting payments or selling paid subscriptions. There is no active checkout or payment-provider buyer portal for Sharflow purchases. Any displayed plan amounts are proposed launch prices, not an offer to purchase.

2. Cancellation and Refund Inquiries

No recurring Sharflow billing is currently available to cancel. If you believe you have been charged for Sharflow, contact hello@sharflow.online so we can investigate. Do not send payment-card details. We do not promise a refund-processing timeline for a payment service that is not active.

3. Before Paid Launch

Final prices, billing intervals, cancellation procedures, payment-provider details and refund terms will be published before paid checkout becomes available. Account approval alone does not activate payment processing.

4. Statutory Consumer Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude or limit any non-waivable statutory rights you may hold under applicable local consumer protection legislation. Where statutory cancellation or withdrawal periods apply by law to digital services before performance has commenced, those provisions take precedence.

5. Contact Support

For account or billing inquiries, contact hello@sharflow.online or visit our contact page.
